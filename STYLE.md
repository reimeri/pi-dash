# UI Style Guide

## Direction

- Keep the interface minimal, restrained, and functional.
- Support a dark theme only.
- Use a shadcn-inspired visual language: neutral colors, subtle borders, modest radii, clear typography, and deliberate spacing.

## Guidelines

- Prefer simple layouts with strong hierarchy and ample breathing room.
- Use a compact neutral palette; reserve accent colors for meaningful actions and state.
- Avoid gradients, decorative effects, excessive shadows, visual clutter, and unnecessary animation.
- Keep components consistent, accessible, and understated.
- Favor subtle hover, focus, and active states over dramatic transitions.
